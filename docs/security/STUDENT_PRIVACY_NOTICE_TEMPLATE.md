## What information we collect

Depending on the features you use, ArcTester may process:

- Account details: email address, password hash, and account status.
- Academic profile: name, college/institution, college code, branch, registration number and postal PIN when supplied.
- Learning activity: practice and exam answers, scores, progress, lesson activity and analytics.
- Security data: session/device metadata, passkey public credentials, encrypted authenticator secret, hashed recovery codes, login history and security audit events.
- Support and request information: details you provide when contacting the operator about your account or privacy request.

We do not need your password in readable form. ArcTester stores a password hash. Authenticator secrets are encrypted and recovery codes are stored as hashes.

## Why we use this information

We use the information to create and secure accounts, verify academic context and access approval, deliver lessons and exams, score work, show progress, prevent abuse, investigate security incidents, respond to support/privacy requests and maintain service reliability.

Student-identifying information is not included in model training datasets by default. Any future use of personal data for a new purpose must be reviewed and disclosed before it begins.

## Security

ArcTester uses access controls, password hashing, session revocation, MFA/passkeys where enabled, scoped MCP keys, rate limiting, encrypted authenticator secrets and security logging. No internet service can promise absolute security. If an incident affects personal information, the operator will assess and make notifications required by applicable law and contract.
